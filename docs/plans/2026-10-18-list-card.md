# A better list card in the chat

## Context
When the chat shows or changes the shopping list it adds a card under the reply. Today that card is an assistant bubble with the whole list as pre-wrapped text (every item, "- Boniato: 1,8 kg"), and only at the very bottom, after 30 lines, the total, the cost per day, the budget line and the prices' date. At 390 px it is 855 px tall, and the thing people care about, the price, comes last. The menu card next to it was just redone as a card ([plan](2026-10-18-menu-card.md)), so this one looks plain beside it.

## Approach
The same card language as the menu card, with the price first. `ListPreview` in `src/List.tsx`, styles in the chat section of `src/index.css`, a few strings.
- **Header:** the badge (here the Lista icon), "Tu lista", and a pill with how many products are left to buy ("23 productos").
- **Price block:** where the total is known, the chosen store's icon (two when the buy is split), the total large in tabular figures, "Todo en Géant" or "Géant y Disco" under it, and the cost per day in muted type. Under that, the budget line as plain ink (over budget is announced but isn't an error, as in the List tab). When no one store has the whole list: the existing sentence, and each store's total in muted type.
- **Sections:** one row per section with what's left to buy (hairlines like the menu card's): the section's name on the left and the number of products on the right in muted tabular figures. The items themselves are in the List tab, one tap away; the card no longer repeats them.
- **Note and button:** the muted line with the products not found and the prices' date, then the "Ver lista" pill with the arrow.
- **Other states:** while prices load, or can't be read, or the menu is old: the header, the sections and one muted line where the price block would be. When there's nothing left to buy: the header and "No te falta comprar nada.".
- **Shared styles:** the menu card and this one share the card, header, badge and button rules, so a change to one is a change to both.

Rejected: keeping the item names, clamped, under each section name. It repeats the tab, and brings the card back to about 600 px.

## Check
`npm run dev:fixture`, the default long chat (priced, inside the budget), `?prices=over`, `?prices=old` and `?prices=error`, at 390 px and wide, in light: height before and after, a two-store option if the fixture has one. `npx tsc -b`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build`.

## Docs
`docs/design.md`: the "List card" bullet in Chat.

## Results
- Built as planned: `ListPreview` in `src/List.tsx`, the shared card styles and the list card's own in `src/index.css`, and the `listCard` strings. The menu card and this one now share the card, header, badge and button rules. `optionText` and the `prices.oneStore` and `prices.split` strings went away with it.
- Checked in `npm run dev:fixture` at 390 px in light:
  - priced and inside the budget (the default chat): 855 px became 553 px, with the total at the top;
  - over budget (`?prices=over`): "No alcanza tu presupuesto de $ 2.500: faltan $ 387." in plain ink;
  - a menu saved before real products (`?prices=old`) and a failed read (`?prices=error`): the header, the sections and one muted line, 416 and 393 px.
- `npx tsc -b`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build` pass.
- Not checked: a two-store split and the "no store has everything" case (the fixture has neither), the "nothing left to buy" state, a wide screen, Safari and a screen reader.
- The items are no longer in the card, only their count per section. If they should come back, the way would be a clamped line of names under each section name, which brings the card back near 600 px.

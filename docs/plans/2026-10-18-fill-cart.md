# Fill the supermarket cart from the list

Status: **PR 1 in review** (steps 1 to 7 done on 2026-10-18; step 2's logged-in test is left to the user; PR 2, steps 9 to 13, not started). Branch `feat/fill-cart`, cut from `main` at `0dd0c6b`, which includes build101-23/app#13 (real products) and build101-23/app#14 (the v3 redesign), with `main` merged in after build101-23/app#15 to #18. Those took ADRs 0028 and 0029, so this plan's ADR is 0030. The branch lives in its own worktree, `.claude/worktrees/fill-cart`, because the main checkout is in use for other work.

## Context
- The list is priced from the products the chat picked ([ADR 0024](../adr/0024-agent-builds-the-menu-from-real-products.md)). The List tab already knows, per line and per store, the `store_sku`, the units of sale to buy (`lineOf(need, offer).packs`), the product's link and its cost, plus which store or split the store rule chose.
- The [cart handoff spike](../notes/cart-handoff-feasibility.md) proved both paths, and [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-fill-carts-from-the-users-computer.md) picked them. Ta-Ta is filled by a VTEX add-to-cart link opened in the user's browser. Disco and Géant are filled by an agent clicking "Agregar" inside the user's logged-in Chrome, since they only keep carts for logged-in users and have no cart link.

What the user asked for (2026-10-18): once the list exists, the cart at the chosen supermarket fills itself, and the user only enters their details and pays. "La idea es que todo el pedido quede cargado automáticamente en el súper en el carrito y listo." For now this is on a computer.

## Decisions (made in chat, 2026-10-18)
- **Scope: one branch, two PRs.**
  - **PR 1:** Ta-Ta by link.
  - **PR 2:** a Chrome extension for Disco and Géant, loaded unpacked on the team's computers.
  - Devoto stays out: the menu has no Devoto picks.
- **Legal, a hackathon assumption** (as [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-agent-builds-the-menu-from-real-products.md) did for prices):
  - The Ta-Ta link may reach pilot users. It's a standard VTEX store feature that the user opens once, in their own browser.
  - The Disco and Géant automation stays on team machines until the legal read ([open decisions](../notes/open-decisions.md)).
- **Buttons, in the v3 design** ([design.md](../design.md#list)), inside the "Dónde comprar" card. A screen's main action goes in its content, and nothing floats over the dock.
  - A *primary* "Armar carrito en {store}" for each store of the chosen option. A split gets two buttons, each with only its own lines.
  - A store this computer can't fill (Disco or Géant without the extension) gets no button, only a muted line: its items keep their product links in the list.
  - A *quiet* "Armar todo en Ta-Ta · $X · faltan N" fills every line Ta-Ta prices, whenever that adds something to the option's Ta-Ta button: the option leaves Ta-Ta out, can't be filled here, or there is no option. In a split where Ta-Ta already gets everything it sells, it would only repeat the same link, so it's hidden.
  - Tangerine is never a call to action.
- **What goes in:**
  - Each line left to buy, at the store the chosen option buys it in, with `lineOf().packs` units of sale: the same "N × product" the line already shows. Crossed-out items stay out; partly-had ones use what's left.
  - Food sold by weight (`size_source = 'unit'`) goes in only at a chain where a test (step 2 or 9) shows what a quantity of 1 means there. Until then it's listed as not added, with its link. Ta-Ta passed in step 2: one unit of sale per `qty`, at our price.
  - **The 14-day rule:** a pick whose `store_products` row is more than 14 days older than that store's newest read is treated as delisted. It's dropped from the price and from the cart, the same rule `search_products` uses at pick time, so the total and the cart always match.
- **A cart that already has items, and a second tap:**
  - Nothing is ever removed.
  - Ta-Ta: the link already works this way (step 2). It keeps the cart's other items, and each sku ends at the larger of what the cart had and what the link asks. A second tap changes nothing, so it needs no confirm. The summary says what was already in the cart stays.
  - Disco and Géant: each of our products is raised to at least our quantity and never lowered, and other items are left alone. A second run adds nothing twice.
- **Before and after:**
  - Under each button, a muted line: "Se agregan N productos · $X estimado, sin envío", then one line per reason something stays out, worded so nothing has to agree with the names: "Ta-Ta no tiene: …", "Agregar a mano (por peso): …". Items no store sells get one line for the whole block: "Ningún súper tiene: …".
  - After the tap, in the always-mounted `role="status"` line:
    - Ta-Ta: "Revisá el carrito en Ta-Ta y pagá ahí."
    - Disco and Géant: "Agregamos 12 de 14 en Disco. Faltan: …", with the run's progress on the busy button ("Armando carrito… 3 de 14").
- **Disco and Géant delivery questions:** the user answers the chain's own dialog the first time. The extension pauses with "Elegí cómo recibir el pedido en la página" and goes on once the dialog closes. The chain remembers the answers per account, and we store nothing: no address, no branch.
- **When the extension can't finish:** it checks the login first and asks the user to log in. It skips unavailable products and caps retries per product. On a real failure it stops and reports what was added and what's missing, never leaving a silent half-filled cart.
- **Out of this branch:**
  - an interest-only "Pedímelo" button, and events or metrics;
  - a weekly automatic fill, a check of each product in the user's zone, and substitutes;
  - phones for Disco and Géant;
  - delivery cost in the store rule.
- **Unchanged:** the list's quantity column, Share and Copy keep the catalog pack ("2 × 1 kg"). The product line under each item is the cart quantity.
- **Chat:** one prompt line sends a user who asks it to order to the List tab's button. The chat never says it filled a cart.

## Design

### Shared: cart lines (`supabase/functions/_shared/cart.ts`, new)
- `cartFor(p: Pricing, store: StoreKey, ids: Iterable<string>): Cart`, with `Cart = { store, lines: CartLine[], skipped: Skipped[], total }`. `optionCarts(p, option)` gives one per store of an option.
  - `ids` is the chosen option's items at that store (`chosen.at[id] === store`). For the "Armar todo en Ta-Ta" alternative, it's every need.
  - `CartLine = { sku, qty, name, url, cost }`, with `qty = lineOf(need, offer).packs`. Two items that picked the same sku become one line with the quantities added up: a sku repeated in one Ta-Ta link keeps only the larger quantity (step 2).
  - `Skipped = { id, reason: 'not_here' | 'weighed' | 'not_found' }`.
- Products sold by weight need the row's `size_source`. Today `offerOf` drops it, so `Offer` gains `weighed` (`size_source = 'unit'`: a piece, tray or bag bought whole). It's separate from the existing `by_weight`, loose food priced on the exact weight. A per-chain switch, `weighedInCart: Record<StoreKey, boolean>`, starts `true` for Ta-Ta (step 2) and `false` for Disco and Géant until step 9.
- `tataCartUrl(lines)`: `https://secure.tata.com.uy/checkout/cart/add?` followed by `sku=…&qty=…&seller=1` per line and `&sc=4` once at the end.
  - The host is `secure.`, not `stores.base_url`, because the storefront has no cart.
  - Seller `1` and channel `4` are constants, with a comment linking the feasibility note.
- `npm test` covers it in `cart.test.ts`, as for `offers.test.ts`:
  - a split, where each store gets only its lines;
  - crossed-out and partly-had items;
  - duplicate skus;
  - the three skip reasons;
  - the URL.

### Shared: the 14-day rule
- A migration adds `store_newest()`: a security-invoker SQL function that returns each store's newest `checked_at` for disco, geant and tata. It's the same as `search_products`' `newest` CTE, and readable by any signed-in user, like the catalog.
- `freshRows(rows, newest)` in `products.ts` drops a row older than its store's newest read minus `FRESH_DAYS = 14`, defined there once. The new migration's comment links to it.
- It runs where the picked products are read, so `pricing()` and everything after it only see listed products:
  - `usePrices` (`src/prices.ts`) reads `store_newest()` with the products;
  - the `chat` function's `productRows` does the same, so the model, `save_menu`'s checks and the screen still agree.
- If `store_newest()` can't be read, no row is dropped: the rule is a filter, and the price still shows.
- Tests for an old row dropped and a fresh one kept.

### PR 1: Ta-Ta in "Dónde comprar" (`src/Cart.tsx`, new, mounted from `src/List.tsx`)
- The component goes at the bottom of `section.prices`, above the date note. It takes `p`, `chosen` and the menu version. All its Spanish copy goes in a new `t.cart` block in `src/strings.ts`.
- The Ta-Ta button is an `<a className="button primary" href={tataCartUrl(lines)} target="_blank" rel="noopener">`:
  - The navigation runs straight from the tap, so nothing can block it (the rule at `src/List.tsx`'s clipboard comment).
  - Tapping it again is harmless, since the link never adds a sku twice (step 2).
  - An unknown sku doesn't break the link: Ta-Ta's cart page names it and adds the rest (step 2).
- The quiet "Armar todo en Ta-Ta" is the same anchor with `button quiet`, with only the lines of what stays out under it, since its label has the total.
- `money` and `nameOf` move from `src/List.tsx` to `src/format.ts`, shared with `src/Cart.tsx`.
- CSS: a `.cart` block in `src/index.css`, from the existing tokens only. If `.button` doesn't already style an `<a>`, add that rule.
- The dev fixture gets the states we need to check: Ta-Ta in the chosen option, a split with Ta-Ta, a list where Ta-Ta isn't chosen, and an item sold by weight.

### PR 2: the Disco and Géant extension (`extension/`, new)
- **Files** (plain JS, no build step, linted by `oxlint`):
  - `manifest.json`: Manifest V3, name "VIVERI carrito (equipo)". A `key` pins the extension ID.
  - Permissions: `scripting` and `storage`, plus host permissions for `https://www.disco.com.uy/*` and `https://www.geant.com.uy/*` only.
  - `externally_connectable.matches`: the deployed app, `http://localhost/*` and, if Chrome accepts it, the preview hosts.
  - `background.js`: the service worker.
  - `gdu.js`: the functions `chrome.scripting.executeScript` runs in the store's tab.
- **Contract:** the page opens a port with `chrome.runtime.connect(EXTENSION_ID)`.
  - `{type:'ping'}` → `{type:'pong', version}`.
  - `{type:'fill', store, lines:[{sku, url, qty, name}]}` → `{type:'progress', done, total, name}` messages → `{type:'result', added, raised, already, unavailable, failed, stopped?}`.
  - The worker checks `sender.origin`, the store, and that each URL is that store's host with a `/product/` path.
  - No Supabase token reaches the extension, and it calls none of our servers.
- **Run:** a step machine whose state lives in `chrome.storage.session`. An MV3 worker sleeps after 30 s idle, and every product is a full page load, which ends any script in the page.
  1. Open the store in a new tab. Check the header for "Mi Cuenta"; if it's missing, ask the user to log in and wait up to 4 min.
  2. For each line, `chrome.tabs.update` to its URL, wait for the load, then run `addToCart(qty)` in the page:
     - "No Disponible": unavailable.
     - The stepper already at `qty` or more: already there.
     - Otherwise click "Agregar", then "add" until the stepper reaches `qty`, with at most `qty + 3` clicks, each waiting for the number to change (8 s timeout).
     - A sign-in dialog ("Identificaci…"): ask the user to log in. Any other dialog: the delivery pause above.
  3. End on `https://<host>/cart` and send the result. Never click "Proceder al pago".
  - Port the spike's logic from `spikes/cart-handoff/disco_cart.py`, but not its bugs: an unbounded quantity loop, `sys.exit` mid-run, unbounded retries after login, and no login check before the first click.
- **Web app:** `src/extension.ts` reads the ID from `VITE_CART_EXTENSION_ID`.
  - `detect()`: `typeof chrome?.runtime?.connect === 'function'`, then a ping with a timeout.
  - `fill(store, lines, onProgress)`.
  - `Cart.tsx` shows the Disco or Géant button only once the extension answers. While the run goes, the button is busy with `aria-busy`.
- **Clicks:** if page clicks from `executeScript` (`isTrusted = false`) don't work on GDU, the fallback is `chrome.debugger` input events. That shows Chrome's "debugging this browser" bar and adds an install warning.
